# Synthos US pricing benchmarks and launch recommendation

**As of July 31, 2026. Prices are USD and generally per business location unless noted.**

## What the market says

| Product | Current benchmark | Implication for Synthos |
|---|---:|---|
| Basic claim/listing | Google Business Profile and Yelp Business Page are free; Yelp explicitly separates a claimed page from paid advertising.[1][2] | A claim fee would add friction and conflict with the dominant market norm. |
| Enhanced/paid directory profile | Local chamber examples charge $50/year, $99/year and $150/year for enhanced/premier profiles; Alignable charges $29, $49, and $89/month for paid networking/visibility tiers.[3][4][5] | A low-price enhanced profile is credible, but value must extend beyond merely being listed. |
| Featured/sponsored visibility | Yelp charges from $150/month for Ads, $180/month for its profile Upgrade Package, or from $270/month combined.[2] | An early directory with limited traffic should launch materially below Yelp, then raise prices only after it can document impressions and leads. |
| Pay per lead | Google Local Services Ads charges only for valid leads; price varies by location, job and lead type, and message leads are generally cheaper than calls.[6] Thumbtack has no entry or annual fee and lets professionals control budgets/lead prices.[7] | Do not combine a mandatory subscription with lead fees at launch. Bill only defined, valid leads and offer credits for invalid ones. |
| Paid acquisition CPL | WordStream/LocaliQ’s 2025 search-ad dataset reports $70.11 average CPL overall, with examples from $28.50 for auto repair and $30.27 for restaurants to $90.92 for home improvement, $100.48 for real estate, and $131.63 for legal.[8] | Category-based lead prices are more defensible than one universal price. Synthos should begin below paid-search CPL because its lead volume and close-rate evidence are unproven. |
| One-time audit | Public anchors run from a $99 entry AEO/GEO audit to a $999 expert local SEO/entity audit; Sterling Sky starts a comprehensive local SEO audit at $2,100. WebFX places one-time local SEO projects, including audits, at $500–$5,000.[11][13][15][17] | A $995 combined local SEO/AEO/GEO audit is a credible early-stage center point if it is human-verified and implementation-ready. |
| Ongoing local SEO | Whitespark publishes $499–$1,999/month; BrightLocal starts managed SEO at $1,299/month; WebFX reports a $500–$3,000/month market average, starts its own service at $575/month, and describes typical service pricing of $300–$2,000/month.[9][10][11][12] | A managed single-location offer around $1,100–$1,300/month is market-aligned rather than bargain-basement. |
| Ongoing AEO/GEO | A productized specialist lists $590, $1,090, $1,990 and $3,490/month; eSEOspace lists combined SEO/GEO/AEO plans from $500 to $3,000/month.[15][16] | Include AEO/GEO in the base remediation offer; do not price it as an enterprise-only add-on before Synthos has case studies. |
| Premium agency ceiling | Sterling Sky starts local SEO for most home-service industries at $4,000/month.[14] | This is an upper-market reference, not an appropriate launch target for Synthos. |

## Recommended launch price card

| Funnel step | Launch price | What is included / guardrail |
|---|---:|---|
| **Auto-added/unclaimed listing** | **$0** | Public NAP, category, website and basic free machine-generated score. Clearly label unclaimed data and provide correction/removal workflow. |
| **Claimed listing** | **$0 forever** | Owner verification, edits, hours, service area, one logo/photo set, contact CTA, review response link and free micro-audit snapshot. Do not imply that claiming improves rank. |
| **Enhanced listing** | **$19/month or $190/year** | Rich description, photos/video, services, social links, offers, analytics and listing-change monitoring. Annual price is 16.7% below 12 monthly payments. |
| **Featured/sponsored listing** | **$79/month or $790/year per category/DMA** | Clearly labeled sponsored placement, maximum three paid slots per category/DMA, prominent CTA, no competitor promotion on the profile, and monthly impression/click/lead reporting. Raise new-sale pricing toward **$149/month** only after a category page reliably produces at least 500 qualified monthly sessions or five attributable leads in 30 days. Never sell “featured” inventory without reporting delivery. |
| **Paid micro-audit upsell** | **$99 one time** | Human-verified review of the free snapshot, one location, up to 10 priority issues, three-competitor check, AI-answer spot check and a short walkthrough. Credit the full $99 toward a full audit bought within 30 days. The free result should diagnose; the paid micro-audit should prioritize. |
| **Full local SEO + AEO/GEO audit** | **$995 one time** | GBP, citations/NAP, reviews, local rank grid, technical/on-page/schema/entity review, AI prompt/citation sample, competitors, prioritized 90-day roadmap and one readout. No implementation. Move to **$1,495** after five credible case studies or when scope routinely exceeds one location/50 pages. |
| **3-month remediation sprint** | **$1,295/month; $3,885 total** | Fix highest-impact technical, GBP, citation, schema and conversion issues; establish baseline measurement. Best default for uncertain buyers. |
| **6-month growth engagement** | **$1,195/month; $7,170 total** | Sprint work plus recurring page optimization, review operations, local authority/citation work and monthly AI/local visibility tracking. |
| **12-month managed growth** | **$1,095/month; $13,140 total** | Same core service with the lowest monthly rate, quarterly strategy resets and compounding content/entity maintenance. |

**Retainer scope discipline:** price includes one location, one primary site, up to two existing-page optimizations per month, citation/review operations, tracking and reporting. Net-new long-form content, paid media, digital PR, premium placements and backlink acquisition are separately scoped or passed through. Credit **50% of the $995 full audit** against a 6- or 12-month engagement; do not stack this with the $99 micro-audit credit.

## Lead-fee recommendation

Launch **exclusive, verified leads only** at three simple category bands:

- **$20/lead — low-value/high-volume:** restaurants, auto repair and similar categories.
- **$50/lead — core local services:** personal services and ordinary appointment businesses.
- **$100/lead — high-value/competitive:** legal, real estate and major home projects.

Set the final category price to the lower of **70% of the current external paid-search CPL benchmark** or **10% of expected first-job gross profit**. Review after every 25 delivered leads. Require prepaid credits but no subscription; suggested opening blocks are $200, $250 and $300 respectively. A valid lead must have usable contact data, requested service, correct DMA, explicit contact intent, and no duplicate from Synthos in the prior 30 days. Credit invalid leads within seven days. If shared leads are ever introduced, price them at no more than half the exclusive rate and disclose the recipient count; the cleaner launch choice is not to sell shared leads.

## Early unit economics guardrails

These are operating targets, not external benchmarks:

- **Enhanced listing:** keep variable servicing at or below **$2/month**; $19 pricing then yields about **89% gross margin** before sales/overhead.
- **Paid micro-audit:** cap delivery cost at **$38** (about 30 human minutes at a $60 loaded hourly cost plus $8 tools); gross margin is about **62%**.
- **Full audit:** cap delivery at **$350** (five human hours at $60 plus $50 tools); gross margin is about **65%**.
- **Retainers:** target **65% gross margin**. With $75/month of tools/pass-through cost and $60 loaded labor, monthly human-delivery caps are approximately **6.3 hours** on the 3-month plan, **5.7 hours** on the 6-month plan and **5.1 hours** on the 12-month plan. If Synthos cannot produce the stated deliverables inside those caps, reduce scope or raise price rather than hide labor in “AI automation.”
- **Lead sales:** target at least **60% contribution margin** after traffic acquisition, validation, credits and payment fees. Pause a category if the rolling 25-lead cohort misses that target or buyers cannot document acceptable contact and close rates.

## Launch judgment

Keep the marketplace free at the identity layer and monetize **measurable prominence, verified diagnosis, implementation and valid demand**. The recommended ladder—$0 → $19/$190 → $79/$790 → $99 → $995 → roughly $1.1K–$1.3K/month—sits below established premium products without signaling low quality. Treat paid listings as secondary revenue until traffic is proven; the near-term economic engine should be audits converting into tightly scoped remediation retainers.
