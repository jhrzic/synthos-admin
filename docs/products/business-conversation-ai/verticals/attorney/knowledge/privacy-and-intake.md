# Privacy and what we ask for before a consultation

Before your appointment we ask for only four things: your name, your contact details, which of our
practice areas your matter falls under, and your relationship to the estate if someone has died.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, account numbers, dates of birth or copies of documents
before you have met an attorney. If a website, email or caller asks you for those in our name,
please telephone the office on (555) 0148-2200 before responding.

Nothing you tell this assistant creates an attorney-client relationship, and it should not be
treated as confidential in the way a conversation with an attorney is. Please do not share detailed
facts about a dispute here. The intake coordinator will take the details when they speak with you.
