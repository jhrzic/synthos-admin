# How a consultation with Harrow & Vance works

Every new matter starts with a 45-minute initial consultation with one of our three attorneys. The
consultation fee is $150, payable at the appointment, and is credited against your first invoice if
you go on to instruct us.

The consultation is a conversation, not a commitment. You will leave with a written summary of the
options available to you and a fee estimate for each. We do not ask for payment beyond the
consultation fee at that meeting.

Consultations are held in person at our Cranmer Street office, or by video call if travelling is
difficult. Telephone consultations are available for existing clients only.

An attorney confirms every appointment personally. Our assistant can take your preferred times and
pass them to the intake coordinator, but a time is not booked until a member of staff confirms it
with you directly.
