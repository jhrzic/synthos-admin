# What to bring to a consultation

## If you are settling the estate of someone who has died

Bring whatever you can find. Nothing on this list is required in order to meet with us, and people
frequently arrive with only one or two items.

- The death certificate, or a copy. If you do not have one yet, the funeral director can usually
  obtain certified copies.
- The original will if you can locate it, along with any codicils. Photocopies are useful if the
  original cannot be found.
- Any trust documents.
- Recent statements for bank, savings and investment accounts held in the deceased's name.
- Property deeds, mortgage statements, or the title for any vehicles.
- Life insurance policies and pension or retirement account paperwork.
- Recent bills, loan statements, or letters from creditors.
- Names and current addresses for the surviving spouse, children and any other beneficiaries named
  in the will.
- Your own photo identification.

## If you are making or updating your own estate plan

- Photo identification.
- A list of your assets and roughly what each is worth.
- The names and addresses of the people you would like to name as beneficiaries, executor, trustee
  and attorney-in-fact.
- Any existing will, trust or power of attorney.

If you cannot find something, come anyway. Part of what the first meeting is for is working out
what exists and what needs to be tracked down.
