# Sleeping hot

Sleeping hot is the single most common complaint we hear, and it is usually the mattress
construction rather than the room.

Traditional memory foam holds heat because it is dense and conforms closely around the body,
reducing airflow. Customers who report waking up hot on an older foam mattress are usually
describing this.

The three constructions we stock that sleep cooler are:

- **Hybrid** — a pocket-spring core with a comfort layer on top. Air moves through the spring unit,
  so it runs noticeably cooler than solid foam while still contouring.
- **Latex** — naturally open-celled, so it breathes well and does not hold heat the way memory foam
  does. It also feels more responsive, which suits people who dislike the sinking sensation.
- **Gel-infused foam** — foam with a cooling gel layer. Cooler than standard memory foam, though
  generally not as cool as a hybrid or latex.

Mattress protectors matter more than people expect. A waterproof vinyl-backed protector will trap
heat and can undo the benefit of a cooler mattress. We stock a cotton terry protector for exactly
this reason.
