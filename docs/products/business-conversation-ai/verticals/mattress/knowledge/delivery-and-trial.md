# Delivery, removal and the comfort trial

## Delivery

Delivery is free within 25 miles of the Northfield showroom. Deliveries run Tuesday to Saturday.
Outside 25 miles we deliver by arrangement and there is a charge, which the showroom will confirm.

Our delivery team takes the new mattress to the room of your choice and sets it up.

## Old mattress removal

Removal of your old mattress is included at no extra charge when we deliver a new one. The old
mattress needs to be stripped of bedding and accessible. We cannot remove a mattress that is wet,
soiled or infested.

## The 100-night comfort trial

Every mattress comes with a 100-night comfort trial. A new mattress needs about two weeks before
your body adjusts, so the trial does not begin in earnest until then.

If it is not right after at least 21 nights, we will exchange it once for another model in the
range. If the replacement costs more, the difference is payable; if it costs less, the difference is
refunded.

The trial is an exchange, not a money-back guarantee. The mattress must be in clean, undamaged
condition and must have been used with a protector.
