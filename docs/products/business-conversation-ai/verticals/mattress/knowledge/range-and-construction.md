# Our range

We stock six models. We are deliberately a small range — we would rather know six mattresses well
than carry forty.

| Model | Construction | Firmness | Typically suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bellamy Foam | Gel-infused memory foam | Medium | Back sleepers who like close contouring and are not troubled by heat |
| Bellamy Foam Firm | Gel-infused memory foam | Firm | Front sleepers, and back sleepers wanting more support |
| Carrow Hybrid | Pocket springs with foam comfort layer | Medium-firm | The most common recommendation for lower back pain |
| Carrow Hybrid Zoned | Zoned pocket springs, firmer under the lumbar region | Medium-firm | Side sleepers with lower back pain |
| Ashgrove Latex | Natural latex over a spring base | Medium-firm | Customers who sleep hot and dislike the sinking feeling of foam |
| Ashgrove Latex Soft | Natural latex over a spring base | Medium-soft | Side sleepers who sleep hot |

All six are available in single, double, king and super king.

Firmness is subjective and depends on body weight. Two people of different weights will describe the
same mattress differently. This is why we ask people to come and lie on them rather than choosing
from a description.
