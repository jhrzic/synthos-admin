# Lower back pain and support

For lower back pain the usual answer is a medium-firm mattress. Too soft and the hips sink out of
line with the shoulders; too firm and the lower back is left unsupported with a gap beneath it.
Medium-firm keeps the spine roughly level.

Sleeping position changes this:

- **Back sleepers** generally do best on medium-firm, which supports the lumbar curve.
- **Side sleepers** usually need slightly softer at the shoulder and hip, with enough contouring
  that those points are not compressed.
- **Front sleepers** need firmer support, as a soft mattress lets the hips drop and arches the lower
  back.

Zoned support is worth knowing about: some of our hybrid models use firmer springs under the hips
and lumbar region and softer springs at the shoulders. Customers with lower back pain who also sleep
on their side tend to get on best with these.

Pillow height matters as much as the mattress and is frequently the real cause. We check pillow
height as part of any showroom fitting.

We are a mattress retailer, not a clinic. We do not diagnose back problems and we do not make
medical claims about any product. Persistent pain is a conversation for a doctor or physiotherapist.
