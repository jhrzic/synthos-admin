## The problem, in the owner's words

- "People ask ChatGPT or Google's AI where to buy a mattress, and it sends them to the chains."
- "Half my website visitors come after we close, ask one question, and leave."
- "I don't have time to post, answer reviews and keep my Google profile up to date."

## What we sell (three steps, each useful on its own)

| Step | Price | What the store gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Free AI visibility check** | $0 | `/check`: three questions a local shopper asks an AI engine, whether the store is named, who is named instead, and which review sites the AI relies on. |
| **Full local + AI visibility audit** | $995 one-time (founding stores: $495) | Google Business Profile, website, reviews, schema, citations, AI answers across a larger question panel, competitors, a 90-day fix list, one walkthrough call. 50% credited against a managed plan. |
| **Showroom Advisor (managed)** | $299/month per location (founding stores: $199 for 6 months) | An AI sleep advisor on the store's website (chat and voice, video avatar optional) that answers from the store's own products and policies, captures leads, books showroom visits and hands off to staff; plus monthly Google profile posts, review-response drafts for the owner to approve, and a monthly visibility re-check. |

Optional add-on once a store is live and asks for it: **managed local SEO/AEO** at $1,095–$1,295/month (see the pricing benchmarks). Do not lead with it.

## What the advisor will and won't do

Taken from the mattress vertical profile (`docs/products/business-conversation-ai/verticals/mattress/profile.json`):

- **Will:** talk about sleep problems in plain language, explain delivery, trial and showroom services, capture name + contact + need, request a showroom visit, and hand off to a person.
- **Won't:** quote prices, confirm stock, promise delivery dates, invent discounts or create urgency. Every conversation opens with an AI disclosure.

## Founding-store terms (first 5 stores)

- Month-to-month after the first 90 days; cancel any time after that with 30 days' notice.
- Setup included: we load the store's products, policies, hours and FAQs.
- The store owns its leads and conversation transcripts; export on request; deleted on cancellation.
- **No performance or earnings promises.** We report what happened: conversations, leads captured, visits requested, AI answers that name the store. (The FTC's 2026 action against Air.ai was over earnings claims; don't make any.)
